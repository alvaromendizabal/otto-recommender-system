# Project scope and completion

The reference experiment and full batch inference are complete. The project is
now in **temporal validation and release preparation**. The active work repeats
the frozen research procedure, then delivers a traceable submission collection
and a final employer-facing release.

The [research progress snapshot](../reports/robustness/progress.json) and
[verified comparison](../reports/robustness/comparison.json) are the sources for
completion counts. A launched job, generated model, or attractive chart does
not count as a verified research result.

The early window's **seed 20260908 is complete and audited**, including its own
historical retrieval and fitting-only screen. The next bounded milestone is
**early seed 20260909**, reusing the verified preparation, then training, evaluating
and auditing its separate models. Seed 20260910 follows, then the three middle-window
seeds. The [frozen protocol](ROBUSTNESS.md) defines what may change and what must
remain fixed. The original model remains the headline result while this study runs.

A submission is the prediction file, not the repository: one CSV row per session
and action, containing up to 20 recommended product IDs. The existing compressed
file contains **5,015,409 rows**. See [download and submission instructions](INFERENCE.md#download-and-submit-the-completed-full-run).
Generating distinct files and obtaining Kaggle acceptance or scores are separate
steps. No Kaggle score has been recorded.

## Optional work after the release

New neural sources within this protocol, sequence models, alternative rankers,
new blending studies and online experiments remain possible extensions. They
are not required to finish the currently defined research and delivery scope.
